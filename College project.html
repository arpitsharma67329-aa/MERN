About Invertis University

About Us

Invertis University is a multidisciplinary university located in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh. The university focuses on quality education, innovation, research and the overall development of students.

Our Vision

To create an excellent learning environment where students can develop knowledge, skills, creativity and leadership qualities.

Our Mission

  • Provide quality education.
  • Encourage innovation and research.
  • Develop professional skills.
  • Promote cultural activities.
  • Encourage sports and extracurricular activities.
